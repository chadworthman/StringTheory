Help

Using the page

  • The bar along the bottom switches between the four screens: Now (what's on, speed and brightness), Shows (everything you can play), Create (make your own patterns and playlists) and Setup (chips for Schedule, Bulbs, Zones and Help).
  • Pull down from the top of the page and let go to refresh it.
  • The zone row under the logo picks which zone the page controls, or All to change every zone at once: a show, power, speed, brightness, the schedule or the hockey team you follow. The switch at the end of the row turns the lights on or off. Zones are renamed or deleted under Zones on Setup.
  • The bulbs at the top show what each string is showing right now, one row per string. They stay in view as you scroll. Below them, a green dot means a string is doing what it should. Amber means it's missing some changes (usually weak Wi-Fi), and red means it isn't responding.
  • Zones group strings so different parts of the house can play different shows on their own schedules. Set them up under Zones on Setup: add, rename and delete zones, pick each string's zone, and say which end each zone starts at, seen from the street, so shows run the way the page draws them. Find new lights there finds lights you've added in the Smart Life app; add each one as a string, then pick its zone.
  • Bulbs (on Setup) fits the lights to your roofline. Tap a bulb's number to turn it Off (for bulbs out of sight) or Join it to the bulb before, so they light up as one (for bulbs that run back around a corner). Shows then run along just the bulbs that are left, at an even pace. Find it on the string lights that bulb white for a moment so you can see which one it is. Saving restarts the lights for a few seconds. Below are the test patterns for checking the strings' order and every bulb.
  • Schedule sets when the lights come on and go off each day: at set times, or around sunset or sunrise (for example 5 minutes before sunset, for 6 hours). Sun times need your rough location: tap Locate me. The schedule can also fade the lights in and out over a few seconds or minutes.
  • Power: the switch in the zone row turns the lights on or off until the schedule would next agree, then hands back to it. While it's holding, Back to the schedule on Now follows the schedule again straight away.
  • Reverse direction (under Speed, for shows that move) runs that show the other way. Each show remembers it. If your shows run the opposite way to the page, set which end your first bulb is at under Bulbs on Setup instead: that fixes every show at once.
  • Brightness dims every show in a zone, from 10% to 100%.
  • Speed appears for shows that move. Each show remembers its own speed. The top speed is where the lights would start skipping bulbs.
  • On Shows, the chips filter to Themes (grouped by occasion; still themes have an Animate switch for a moving version), Hockey teams, Playlists that switch between themes, and Yours (patterns you've saved). On a zone of just a few lights, such as a pair of floods, still themes step through all their colours, and each card has a bar of the show's colours.
  • Hockey: tap a team to play its colours. Tap Follow games on a team instead and your regular show plays as usual, but during each of the team's games the lights switch to its colours, and every goal sets off a red goal light and flashes the team's colours, then the lights go back to your show. Set a Goal delay to match your TV, and use Test celebration to try it. Following a team is the only thing that uses the internet: it reads the NHL's public live scores.
  • Apple Home and Siri: String Theory can join the Home app as Christmas Lights (set it up under Apple Home in Setup, Zones). "Hey Siri, turn on the Christmas lights", "turn them off", "set the Christmas lights to 40%", or ask for any show by name: "turn on Grinch Christmas Lights", "turn on Moving Grinch Christmas Lights". It covers every zone, and on and off hold until the schedule next agrees, like the switch on the page.
  • Patterns made on Create can use the controllers' own built-in effects, which fade smoothly. Each is listed by what it does on NOMA C9 lights; you can rename any of them in the editor.

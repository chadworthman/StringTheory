    Following

    Tap a team to play its colours. Tap Follow games and your regular show plays as usual, but the team's show takes over during each of its games, celebrating every goal with a red goal light and the team's colours, then hands back.

    Goals come from the NHL's live scores, a few seconds after they happen. TV and streams usually run behind, so set the delay until the lights match your screen.

        Where each string hangs

        This draws your house in the bulbs at the top. Strings run end to end along a row, sit between another string's bulbs (every other bulb), or are flood lights you place along the house. It only changes the picture, not the shows.

          Turn off bulbs that are out of sight, or join bulbs so they light up as one, such as bulbs that run back around a corner. Shows then run along the bulbs that are left. Tap a bulb to change it.

            Zones

            A zone is a group of strings with its own name, show, speed, power and schedule. Each string belongs to one zone. Saved patterns and playlists work in every zone.
